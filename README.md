# Hong Kong Supermarket

A responsive business website developed to establish a dedicated online presence for a local Asian grocery store in Winterville, North Carolina.

**Live Website:** [Hong Kong Supermarket](https://hkmarketgreenvillenc.com/)

## Project Overview

Hong Kong Supermarket had existing Google Business Profile and Apple Business Connect listings but did not have a dedicated website.

I developed a website to provide customers with a centralized source of information about the supermarket, its products, and its location. In addition to frontend development, I updated the business's existing online listings and personally photographed the store and merchandise to create original website content.

My work combined website development, local search visibility, photography, and content preparation to establish a more comprehensive online presence.

## The Business Problem

Although customers could discover Hong Kong Supermarket through Google and Apple business listings, the business lacked a dedicated website where visitors could explore its offerings and access useful store information.

The project presented an opportunity to:

- Establish a dedicated business website.
- Provide customers with accessible store information.
- Showcase merchandise through original photography.
- Connect the website with the business's existing online listings.
- Create a responsive experience for desktop and mobile visitors.

## My Solution

I developed a responsive website using HTML, CSS, and Jekyll, while also improving the business's existing digital presence.

### Website Development

- Developed the website using HTML, CSS, and Jekyll.
- Created a responsive layout that accommodates desktop and mobile devices.
- Implemented responsive navigation.
- Integrated Google Maps to help customers locate the store.
- Developed a product gallery page to showcase available merchandise.
- Created an FAQ section to address common customer questions.

### Photography and Content Creation

One of the most involved parts of the project was creating and preparing original photography for the website.

My responsibilities included:

- Photographing the physical store and its merchandise.
- Selecting and organizing photographs for the website.
- Cropping and editing images for consistent presentation.
- Optimizing image assets for web delivery.
- Preparing product photography for the website's gallery page.

This provided the website with authentic visual content specific to the business rather than relying entirely on generic stock photography.

### Local Search Visibility

The supermarket already maintained Google and Apple business listings. I updated these existing listings to better connect them with the new website.

- **Google Business Profile:** Updated business photographs and website link.
- **Apple Business Connect:** Updated business photographs and website link.

These updates helped maintain consistency between the supermarket's website and its existing business listings.

## Project Challenges and Lessons Learned

### Photography and Image Optimization

Preparing product photography for the website required significant time and attention.

Photographing merchandise was only the first step. Each image also needed to be reviewed, cropped, edited, and optimized before being incorporated into the product gallery.

This process reinforced the importance of image preparation in frontend development, particularly when building visually oriented websites for retail businesses.

It also provided practical experience balancing image quality, consistency, and web performance.
